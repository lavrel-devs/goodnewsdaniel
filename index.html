GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we start.

A few practical details, upfront.

How much does a website cost?+

It depends on the scope, number of pages, content and functionality. After a short conversation, I’ll suggest a realistic scope and quote before work begins.

How long will it take?+

A focused landing page usually takes less time than a multi-page website. The timeline depends on scope and how quickly content and feedback are provided; I’ll agree a schedule with you first.

Can you include WhatsApp enquiries?+

Yes. For suitable projects, I can make it easy for visitors to start a WhatsApp conversation or use a clear enquiry form.

Do you write all the content?+

I can help organise and refine your messaging. We’ll agree whether copywriting, photography, branding and other assets are included in the project scope.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance?+

We can discuss support and updates separately. Hosting, domain registration and ongoing maintenance should be clearly agreed rather than assumed to be included.
